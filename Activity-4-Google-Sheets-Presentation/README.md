## Activity Description
This activity involved preparing a PowerPoint presentation and reporting on our assigned topic as a group. It required us to gather information, organize key points and present the topic clearly to the class.
## What Have I Learned?
I learned how to organize information into clear and understandable presentation slides and how to identify the important points to include in a report. One challenge was making sure that the information was properly arranged and easy for the audience to understand. Working with my group helped us share ideas and divide responsibilities. I can apply these skills in future academic presentations and improve my confidence in communicating information in front of others.
